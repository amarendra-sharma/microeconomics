Everything a student in a professor-led section gets, organized around the pace you set.

The complete textbook

All 17 chapters of the MacroNations Introduction to Microeconomics text, written for a first university course and to be read on your own, with real-world cases, worked examples and interactive figures. Reading progress counts toward your grade.

Live classes with Asha

On the days and at the time you pick, Asha teaches each class live: she explains the material out loud, works problems on a whiteboard and takes your questions by voice or text. Every one of the 92 sections is covered, in order.

Questions answered any time

Stuck on marginal revenue at midnight? Ask Asha between classes, as often as you like. She knows the textbook, your schedule and where you are in the course.

Graded chapter quizzes

A quiz for every chapter, drawn from a bank of questions at three levels of difficulty. Each question carries its own numbers, and every quiz is graded on the server after you submit.

Twelve interactive arenas

Hands-on labs where you do what economists and managers do: clear a market, measure elasticity, find who really pays a tax, pick the best bundle, trace cost curves, set a monopoly price, play an oligopoly game and fix an externality. Each one teaches first, then tests you.

A midterm at the halfway point and a cumulative final, each open for a window of several days so you can choose when to sit them. Both are taken on camera, like the quizzes.

How it works

Four steps, then Asha takes it from there.

Create your account

Sign up as a student with your email. Leave the course join code blank.

Pay the course fee

Start self-study from your Home page and pay once, securely through Stripe. Payment is confirmed automatically.

Design your course

Pick the length (4 to 16 weeks), your first day, the days and time of your live classes, their length and your time zone. You can change it until the first class.

Learn, then certify

Attend Asha’s classes, read, practise in the arenas, take the quizzes and exams, and download your certificate when you finish.

Proctored, so it counts

Every graded quiz and exam is taken on camera.

Your certificate means something because the work behind it is verified. Each graded quiz and both exams run with MacroNations integrity monitoring.

  • A photo-ID check on camera before you begin
  • Fullscreen mode; leaving the window or switching tabs is recorded
  • Copying and pasting are blocked during the session
  • Snapshots when a rule is broken and a short clip if talking is detected, deleted after 30 days
  • Repeated violations submit the quiz or exam automatically
What you need

A laptop or desktop and a quiet room.

  • A computer with a webcam and microphone, using an up-to-date Chrome or Edge browser
  • A government or student photo ID
  • A calculator and one blank sheet of scratch paper for quizzes and exams
  • Comfort with high-school algebra and reading graphs; no prior economics is needed

Questions

Frequently asked

Who is Asha?

Asha is the MacroNations AI professor. She has been built around this course’s textbook and teaches it the way an instructor would: she presents each class, works examples, answers questions, sets your deadlines, grades and gives feedback. She is an AI, not a person.

How long does the course take?

You choose: 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 or 16 weeks, with classes on the days that suit you. Shorter courses mean more classes per week. Your schedule needs at least 12 live classes in total.

Can I change my schedule?

Yes, as often as you like until your first live class begins. After that the schedule is fixed so your deadlines and exam windows stay consistent.

Do I need to attend every class live?

Attendance counts for 5% of your grade, so it pays to come. Between classes you can always ask Asha to go over anything you missed.

Why are quizzes and exams on camera?

So the certificate reflects your own work. The monitoring checks your ID, keeps the session in fullscreen and records any rule breaks. Recordings are kept only for review and are deleted after 30 days.

How long do I keep access?

Your course stays open for 30 days after your final exam window closes, so you can review your work, your feedback and download your certificate.

My professor uses this course. Is self-study the same thing?

Self-study is for independent learners. If your professor gave you a join code, enter it when you sign up instead; your professor’s section has its own schedule and exams.
